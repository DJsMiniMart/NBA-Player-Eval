        Card tiers

        Each card's rating (OVR) comes from where his production per game (PTS + 2×AST + 2×REB + 3×STL + BLK − 4×TOV, the same formula the card game scores) ranks among qualified players this season, so minutes count: a 35-minute starter rates above an efficient 15-minute backup, on a 2K-style scale: the league's best is a 99, a typical rotation player is in the low 70s. "Early" means he hasn't played enough yet to qualify, so his rating is pulled toward average until he has.
