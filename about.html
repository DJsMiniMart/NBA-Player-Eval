About the stat

What is Pulse?

A pulse is how you know someone is alive. Pulse measures how alive a player is for every minute he's on the floor: how much he does to help his team win each possession.

The formula

(PTS + AST×2 + REB×2 + STL×4 + BLK − TOV×4) ÷ MIN Everything a player adds in a game, minus his turnovers, divided by the minutes he played
StatWorthWhy
Points×1The baseline: every point counts once.
Assists×2An assist creates a basket for someone else. Passing is worth more than the box score usually gives it credit for.
Rebounds×2A defensive rebound finishes a stop; an offensive rebound buys another shot. We tested halving defensive rebounds, and it made Pulse worse at predicting wins, so they stay at ×2.
Steals×4A steal ends the other team's possession and usually starts a fast break: a two-possession swing.
Blocks×1A block stops a shot, but the other team often keeps the ball.
Turnovers−4The mirror image of a steal: a possession thrown away, often leading to easy points the other way.
Minutes÷Everything is per minute, so a 12-minute bench spark and a 38-minute star can be compared fairly.

Pulse's signature is the heavy weight on possessions. Most box-score stats are built around scoring; Pulse says that winning and losing the ball matters as much as putting it in the basket.

The Pulse family

Pulse+

Pulse that also charges missed shots: −1 per missed field goal, −0.5 per missed free throw. A player breaks even on twos at 33% and on threes at 25%, so a 10-for-30 night costs him.

Clutch Pulse

The same formula using only what a player does in crunch time (the last 5 minutes of the 4th quarter or overtime with the score within 5), divided by his crunch-time minutes. No crunch-time minutes = 0.

Pulse$

Value per dollar: Pulse production per $1M of salary. "Plays like" shows what the league pays for production like his, and pay tiers compare players with others paid about the same, so stars are measured against stars.

Team Pulse

A team's total production per 48 minutes, and the margin over its opponents, which is what powers the game picks.

Does it work?

Every test below uses only what was known before each game, the same way the picks on The Morning Press are made. There's no hindsight.

68%

Game winners

Team Pulse ratings plus home court picked the winner of 68% of 2025-26 games. Sportsbook favorites typically win about 67–70%.

87.7%

Pulse+ in the box score

The team with the higher Pulse+ in a game won 87.7% of 2025-26 games (Pulse: 83.8%). Missed shots matter.

0.84

Season win %

Over a season, a team's Pulse margin tracks its winning percentage with a 0.84 correlation (1.00 would be perfect).

What Pulse doesn't see

  • Defense beyond steals and blocks. A great position defender who contests and rotates without gambling looks average.
  • Shooting efficiency in plain Pulse (that's what Pulse+ is for).
  • Pace and teammates. Faster teams create more chances to pile up numbers.
  • Small samples. A few minutes can produce wild numbers, so leaderboards use minimum games and minutes.
