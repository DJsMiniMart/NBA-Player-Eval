The 2027 lottery: "3-2-1"

New for 2027-29, approved by the NBA's Board of Governors on May 28, 2026. Full lottery odds and every pick's chances →

16 teams, all drawn

Every lottery pick, 1 through 16, is drawn. The 14 playoff teams pick 17-30 by record.

3 balls

Teams that miss the playoffs and the play-in (except the three worst) get three balls each: the best odds.

2 balls

The three worst records are "draft relegated" to two balls, as are the No. 9 and 10 play-in seeds.

1 ball

The losers of the 7-vs-8 play-in games get one ball each.

No repeats

No team can pick No. 1 two years in a row or land in the top 5 three years running.
